# Change the fonts

- Headings: **Sora** (in place of Space Grotesk).
- Body text: **Manrope** (in place of DM Sans).
- Marathi lines: **Tiro Devanagari Marathi** for headings, in place of Baloo 2. It's a sharper, more editorial Marathi typeface. The Puneri Patya boards switch to **Mukta ExtraBold** so they keep the bold black signboard look.

The site's words, colours and layout stay the same.

## Technical notes
- Update the Google Fonts link in the root page head.
- Update `--font-display`, `--font-sans`, `--font-marathi` in styles.css, and add `--font-patya` for the patya-board utility.
- Update the font line in project memory.
